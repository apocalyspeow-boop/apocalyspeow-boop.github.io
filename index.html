The graph shows your position over the last 6 seconds: low is chin at the bar, high is hanging. The orange line is the top threshold (a rep counts when you dip below it) and the grey line is the re-arm threshold (you must rise above it before the next rep can count). If reps are missed, lower the re-arm line. If extra reps appear, raise it or lower the top line.
